Hover hint on a glass surface. Holds no actions and no links.

```jsx
<Tooltip label="Rotate key"><IconButton icon="arrows-clockwise" label="Rotate key" /></Tooltip>
<Tooltip mono label="2026-07-31 14:02 UTC"><span>3 m ago</span></Tooltip>
```

Pairs with the relative-time convention: relative in the cell, absolute in the tooltip.
