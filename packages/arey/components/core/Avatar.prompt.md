Identity chip. Falls back to two-letter initials in the sans face.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Riya Menon" />
<Avatar name="arey-web" square tone="accent" size="lg" />
<AvatarStack people={members} max={4} />
```

People are round; projects and orgs are `square`. `AvatarStack` overlaps by 8px with a 2px card-coloured ring.
